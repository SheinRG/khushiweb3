    What happens when you send?

    1. 1Your browser builds the transaction and hashes it (SHA-256).
    2. 2It signs the hash with your private key (ECDSA). Only you can do this.
    3. 3The node checks the signature, balance and nonce, adds it to the mempool and gossips it to peers.
    4. 4A miner puts it in a block, finds the proof of work, and every node appends that block.

    How nodes agree

    Every node keeps its own full copy of the chain and checks every block itself. New transactions and blocks are broadcast to peers.

    If two miners find a block at the same time, the network briefly forks. The chain with the most cumulative proof of work wins, and transactions from the losing block go back into the mempool.
